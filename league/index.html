Get started
1. Pick your format below (5v5 / 7v7 / 9v9 / 11v11).
2. Add your players — import the roster sheet the region emailed you, or type them in under Roster & availability.
3. Import schedule: MatchTrak teams just pick their team from the built-in list; region-site teams paste their team calendar link.
4. Pick the game from the dropdown, hit Auto-fill whole game, then drag names to fix positions.
Everything saves in this browser on this device only — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Two things that means:
• Other devices won't see it. If you set up on your laptop, this page on your phone starts empty. To move the team to another device, tap Hand-off code, text it to yourself, and paste it into the Roster panel there. Print from whichever device has your lineup.
• Clearing your browser's history/site data erases it. Keep a copied Hand-off code in a note as your backup.
